FAQ / TROUBLESHOOTING
Why is the waveform flat?

Ensure you are on localhost or HTTPS. Browsers block mic access on file://. Click START.

How do I calibrate?

Click Calibrate. Stay silent for 10 seconds. This builds the robust baseline model.

What is m²?

Mahalanobis distance. It measures how "strange" the current sound is compared to the calibration.
