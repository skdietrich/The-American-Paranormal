Operating Procedures & Technical Documentation

ULTIMATE v3.3 ANOMALY LAB

The flagship instrument of the suite. It combines robust multivariate statistical analysis with conformal prediction models to detect environmental anomalies that deviate from a baseline.

Standard Operating Procedure

  • Calibration: Upon loading, click "CALIBRATE" and remain silent for 10 seconds. The system learns the "Room Tone" (background noise floor) to create a mathematical model of "Normal."
  • Monitoring: Watch the m² (Mahalanobis Distance). A score under 2.0 is normal. Sudden spikes > 10.0 indicate a statistical anomaly.
  • P-Value: This measures rarity. If the P-value drops below 0.01 (1%), the system will flag a "CONFIRMED ANOMALY."

MAX v2.6 SPECTRUM ANALYZER

A precision audio visualizer using Fast Fourier Transform (FFT) to break sound into individual frequencies.

Interpretation Guide

  • Spectrogram (Top): Shows live frequency strength. Look for spikes in the "Ultrasonic" (right side) or "Infrasound" (left side) ranges that coincide with questions.
  • Waterfall (Bottom): A history log of sound. Voice phenomena often appear here as distinct, organic shapes contrasting against the static mechanical noise of the room.

VOX v1.0 DIGITAL MEDIUM

An entropy-based dictionary tool. It monitors the microphone for fluctuations in sound pressure and chaos.

Theory of Operation

When the environmental entropy exceeds a specific threshold (Standard Deviation > 15), the system acts as a random number generator to select a word from an internal database. The theory is that entities can manipulate entropy to "stop" the wheel on a specific word.

VISUS v1.0 OPTICAL SCRYING

A generative fluid dynamics engine designed for visual ITC experiments.

Protocol

  • Darken the room completely.

TROUBLESHOOTING & FAQ

System says "Secure Context Required"
Modern browsers block microphone access on insecure websites. You must access this portal via HTTPS (Github Pages) or localhost. Do not open the HTML files directly from your hard drive (file://).
I don't hear any sound.
By default, audio monitoring is OFF to prevent feedback loops. If you want to hear what the microphone hears, wear headphones, then check the "Monitor Audio" box in the settings panel.
What is a "Good" Anomaly Score?
In ULTIMATE, a Mahalanobis score (m²) of 2-5 is standard background noise. A score of 20+ is significant. A score of 100+ is extreme and warrants immediate investigation.
Can I run this on mobile?
Yes. All apps are optimized for mobile processors. However, ensure your phone is not in "Silent Mode" if you wish to hear the VOX speech synthesis.
